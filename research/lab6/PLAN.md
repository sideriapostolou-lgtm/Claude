# Lab 6: the sports specialist. Pre-registration, version lab6-v1

Written 2026-10-09 ~21:05 UTC. At the time of writing NO Polymarket price had been compared with any Pinnacle line:
the data work so far read team names, start times and print COUNTS only (the inventory in `INVENTORY.md`). Owner
direction (same day): each desk should be a specialist, "insanely good at its own market". Lab 6's market is the
sports game moneyline on Polymarket ("Team A vs. Team B", and the soccer home / draw / away markets).

The specialist's claim, stated before the data: **the sharpest bookmaker in the world (Pinnacle) prices game
winners better than Polymarket's crowd; after removing Pinnacle's margin, a Polymarket side priced clearly below that
fair probability is a positive-expectation bet, even after Polymarket's fee.** If Polymarket's sports prices already
track Pinnacle within the fee and the spread (the efficient-market answer, and what lab 4 found for "near-certain"
sports outcomes), the answer is NO EDGE, and that is a useful answer. Paper only; real money only after TEST passes
AND the owner flips it; a TEST pass never funds real money by itself (labs 2-4's rule).

## 2. Fair probability and timing (FIXED)

- **Fair probability, declared method: multiplicative (proportional)**: implied q_i = 1 / decimal odds, fair p_i =
  q_i / sum(q). Soccer uses Pinnacle's 3-way line (home / draw / away); a Yes side's fair is its result's p, a No
  side's is 1 - p. **Declared alternative: power** (p_i = q_i^k, k solving sum = 1), evaluated on the same grid and
  reported, never selected.
- **No lookahead:** a print at time t may only use the last Pinnacle snapshot whose own timestamp is at or BEFORE t
  and strictly before the game's start. **Maximum staleness: 30 minutes** (a print more than 30 min after the latest
  snapshot has no fair price and is skipped). Snapshots are sparse (§1), so the backtest sees the moments right after
  each snapshot, not every print; coverage per window is reported.
- **Pre-game only:** an entry print must be before the cutoff, min(Pinnacle commence, Polymarket gameStartTime).
- **Closing line:** the last snapshot before the entry cutoff, no older than 30 minutes (else no CLV for that
  game).

## 3. Hypotheses

**H1 (selectable): buy the Polymarket side priced below Pinnacle's fair by more than a margin.**

- Candidate prints: buyable prints for a side (lab 4 Amendment 3: a BUY on the side's token at p, or a SELL on the
  other token at 1 - p) inside the entry window [commence - W, cutoff), with a usable snapshot (§2), and print size >=
  our share count (the print showed at least our $20 at that price).
- **Execution price: the print price plus one tick (0.01, polymarket.com's tick for 0.04-0.96)**, capped at 0.99.
- **Fee:** polymarket.com's taker fee as lab 4 models it (Amendment 1): shares x rate x p x (1 - p), rate from the
  market's fee family (sports 0.05). Edge per share = fair - p_exec - rate x p_exec x (1 - p_exec).
- **Signal:** edge > m. **One bet per game:** the earliest signal across all the game's sides (ties: larger edge).
  $20 ticket, held to resolution, settled at the side's final price. Net per $ = (payout - fee - 20) / 20.
- **Grid:** method {multiplicative (selectable), power (reported)} x W {24 h, 6 h, 1 h} x m {0.01, 0.02, 0.03,
  0.05}: 24 cells, 12 selectable.

**H2 (diagnostic, decides nothing): closing-line value.** Does the price paid beat Pinnacle's last pre-game line?
CLV = closing fair - p_exec (probability points). Reported (mean, event-bootstrap CI95, share > 0) for every H1 cell,
and for a no-skill baseline: every side of every matched game bought at its first ticket-sized buyable print once
the window opens, at print + one tick, no fair-value filter (shows whether Polymarket's pre-game prices lean away from
Pinnacle's close at all, and by how much).

## 4. Selection, bar and splits (FIXED)

- **Bar = lab 4's `core.qualifies`, unchanged:** n >= 100 bets, mean net per $ > 0, event-bootstrap CI95 lower bound >
  0 (B = 2000; a game is one event), at least one loss, and with fewer than five losses the rule-of-three worst case
  must be positive.
- **TRAIN:** shortlist at most TWO selectable cells: the qualifying ones with the highest mean net. None = NO EDGE
  (stop; VAL and TEST are not run).
- **VAL:** each shortlisted cell, once: it must qualify again AND beat lab 4's calibration placebo (outcomes redrawn as
  Bernoulli(price paid), 2000 draws; the real mean at or above the 95th percentile). If both pass, the one with the
  higher TRAIN mean is selected (VAL does not rank). None passes = FAIL on VAL.
- **TEST:** one look at the selected cell only, in a separate step after a review.
- **Robustness readings (reported, never decision inputs):** the power-method twin of every cell; for shortlisted and
  VAL cells, the lab-4 fill (the first buyable print for the same side >= 10 s after the signal, within 600 s, at its
  own price, no extra tick).

## 5. Readings (per cell)

exactly right), realised mean net per $ with event-bootstrap CI95, $ per $20 ticket, total $, CLV mean / CI / share
beating the close, daily Sharpe-like ratio and max drawdown, by-league breakdown; coverage of the windows; the H2
baseline.

## 7. What a pass would and would not mean

A pass says: over these months, Polymarket game prices sat below Pinnacle's no-vig line often and far enough that
buying them paid after polymarket.com's fee and a one-tick slip. It does not say the depth was there for more than
$20, that the next months will repeat, or that the owner can trade it: polymarket.com is close-only from the United
States; the owner's venue is Polymarket US (fee 0.0695 x p x (1 - p), lab 4 Amendment 2), so a pass is re-costed at
that rate before any paper desk, and the live version needs a live Pinnacle feed (The Odds API live odds, 1 credit per
call per region).
