## Exact guarantee and limits

Define floor = min(Up,Down) - (all bought principal + all actual fees). Trigger on min>EPS, abs(Up-Down)<=EPS and floor>EPS. A fee-free-looking pair that is zero/negative after fees does not satisfy this proposed trigger. EPS handling must match existing inventory conventions, including 0.01-contract venue fills.

Once triggered and durably retained, each newly authorized ordinary intent must be strictly balancing confirmed inventory, with aggregate same-side active reservations no larger than the actual imbalance and reserved unit cost strictly below $1. All existing cash, fee, budget, pair-cap, stop, health, owner-window, cutoff and transport checks remain. For a balancing fill of q at fee-inclusive unit u<1 and no overhedge, the outcome floor improves by q*(1-u). At exact balance no further buy is strictly balancing. This is a deterministic permission/exposure restriction, conditional on authentic fills, reserved fee bounds, ordinary venue payouts and existing reconciliation integrity—not a profit guarantee.

If there are no remaining old liabilities and the inventory is positive/full at trigger, refusing all new purchases preserves that positive payout under either outcome. If old orders still exist, their late fills can make the window directional and losing. Cancellation request,200 ack or404 is not release proof; existing reconciliation must retain liability. The change cannot recover past loss, make an initial unmatched buy safe, guarantee a hedge fill, or guarantee nonnegative session/day/account P&L. Do not advertise the selected +.30 as a certified alternative historical result.

## Minimal architecture and persistence

1. Central risk-book policy, not a controller-only filter: mark the event in record_fill immediately after holdings update, plus evaluate restored/current inventory. Admission rejects ordinary nonbalancing proposed buys when protected. Initial buys and pre-pair policies stay unchanged.
2. Retain a per-window irreversible protection marker. Never clear it because a subsequent race fill destroys balance, because a financial stop is resumed, or because UTC day changes. Restriction ends with that window; settlements retain historical accounting.
3. Add a top-level validated set/list of protected window slugs to saved book state, or equivalently robust versioned event evidence. A top-level field avoids changing WINDOW_PARTS, whose exact shape is required for old archive chunks in _merge_archive. Validate types, duplicates/unknown windows and archive restoration. Existing rollback ignores unknown keys and therefore withdraws the new protection; disclose rollback scope.
4. Trigger per reconciled fill, not only at the end of _decide/step. A batch can pass through a full pair before a later fill increases one side. Current reconcile iterates venue-returned fills without sorting; dict insertion order is not an execution chronology. A trigger discovered while applying an arbitrary batch is a confirmed-subset protection event, not necessarily proof the full account was physically flat then; conservative extra restriction is acceptable if described.
5. For legacy restoration, current full-pair inventory is certain. Historical fill-prefix reconstruction requires explicit authoritative chronological ordering, ties and split-fill rules; a deterministic arbitrary tie-break does not certify physical historical order. Do not silently claim all past pair events recovered. Prefer a clearly stated starts-on-confirmation guarantee with persisted new events; if root elects historical replay, test reordered/same-timestamp/split fills and disclose conservative over-trigger or missed-event limitations. A positive exposure floor without exact equality is a different policy and must be named if chosen.
6. Crash window: reconciliation checkpoints money changes, but a crash can occur after observing the pair and before save. Startup cancels/restores/reconciles before new orders. Prove that resupplied fills/current inventory reconstruct protection even if venue fill order differs, or add minimal durable protection evidence. A marker that exists only in RAM cannot support an uninterrupted restart guarantee.
7. _send_guard must recheck protected && !strictly_balancing_intent under transport lock, after wait. Do not call can_buy directly on a reserved intent: its own reservation causes order_pending/double counting. Centralize the protected-intent predicate if useful.
8. _cancel_unsafe should cancel every ordinary residual that is not strictly balancing under aggregate reservations. Reuse the existing cancellation retry/404/uncertainty machinery. Locks remain excluded. Recompute after every cancel yield/partial fill. Preserve uncertain IDs, journal and reservations until existing terminal proof.
9. Keep control/twin distinction honest. The copied-order twin will receive fewer copies/cancels; standalone practice and arena genes are not automatically an exact new-policy counterfactual. Do not change their fill assumptions or silently relabel control identity.

## Adverse cases and forgone gains

A forgoes profitable later rounds and directional winners after the first pair. It may stop after a tiny positive fractional pair while a larger first-order remainder is still pending. The initial full-pair event could be followed by residual fills before cancellation; saved protection must survive this. A batch's uncertain time order can trigger earlier/more often than physical full balance. Old legacy windows may lack proof of an earlier transient pair. After a race makes inventory unmatched, preserving an ordinary strictly balancing bid can still reduce eventual winning-side profit even though it improves worst-case outcome. No-trade risk restriction is not expected-value dominance.

B forgoes directional winning payouts if the cheap hedge fills and then the first side wins; it can miss all fills, suffer queue resets from repricing, and hold cash/budget capacity while resting. Reducing quote price increases modeled edge but does not establish execution probability or expected-value improvement. Do not exempt B from ordinary budgets/paircap/cutoff or use late-lock exemptions early.
