## Design consequence

Startup-only full + resting/recent is still unsafe. A first full portfolio audit plus narrowed second pass remains an **unproved offline coverage experiment**, not an equivalent replacement. The nullable last_update_time field does not remove the between-pass old terminal correction counterexample. status=resting all-time completeness is explicitly stronger evidence for currently resting rows, but it does not prove absence of terminal unknown creates or past owner activity. max_ts does not turn a paginated read into a frozen snapshot. Known-ID404 must fail closed, retaining uncertainty/reservation and legacy fallback.

The current code's unfiltered portfolio audit means complete LIVE DATASET under the current API, not complete lifetime history. This is an independent retention limit of the baseline, not evidence a performance patch caused loss and not evidence this account has crossed any cutoff. Do not silently enlarge PR67 or a scan optimization into archive recovery. Before any claim that all startup prior-prefix history/fills is covered, inspect authorized cutoff evidence and design an explicit live+historical handoff audit separately. No account cutoffs were queried here; no missing actual rows are asserted.

Required new fake cases: archive rollover between first/second legs; a known-ID404 for an archived order; old terminal row outside live endpoint but inside historical; late backfilled fill near cutoff; duplicate/conflicting rows across live/historical; handoff gaps; cursor pages changing while orders cancel/fill; min_ts both creation and update models, inclusivity and delayed visibility. An archive-aware source proposal would require its own explicit scope and review; none is implemented or approved by this note.
